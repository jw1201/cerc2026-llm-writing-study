# Intervention Taxonomy (Types A-G)

| Type | Label | Description | 
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Prompt Iteration | Reformulation of the prompt without directly editing the output |
| B | Factual Correction | Correction or removal of factually incorrect content |
| C | Structural Decision | Changes to order, weighting or outline of the content |
| D | Quality Rejection | Complete rejection of AI output and regeneration of the response |
| E | Content Addition | Addition of own arguments or facts not provided by the AI |
| F | Style Correction | Linguistic revision without content changes |
| G | Information Challenging | Critical questioning and verification of AI-provided information |
