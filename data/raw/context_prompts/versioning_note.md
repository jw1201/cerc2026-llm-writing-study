# Note on Context Prompt Versioning

Twelve versions of the persistent context prompt were used throughout the writing process, updated after each completed step to reflect progress and refine the study design.

## Step Renumbering and Refinement

The step framework was initially defined as a 11-step process, but was later revised during the writing process to include a new step (Step 4: Study Design, Implementation & Analysis), resulting in a 12-step framework. Accordingly, the existing work steps AS4–AS11 were moved back one position. In addition, several steps were sharpened to better clarfiy their purpose and scope. For example, Step 8 changed from Discussion to Discussion, Conclusion & Limitations.
